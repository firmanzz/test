## How the agent should use this catalogue

1. Read the user's intent, then pick the form by **stream** and **stage** (see the decision guide at the end).
2. Every form is linked to the R&D Manual by code (header shows `SOP Reference: ABS-RND-SOP-001`).
3. Filling or completing a form is **never** an authorisation to act. Sign-off / approval is a **human gate**; the final decision stays with Direksi.
4. When a form's data is missing, tell the user which form and which mandatory field is needed — do not invent values.

## The three R&D streams

| Stream | Forms | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Product Validation** (external vendor products) | FRM-RND-01 → 06 | Scout → due diligence → test plan → test log → verdict → register |
| **B. In-House Development** (own products) | FRM-RND-10 → 15 | Idea → dev plan/gates → prototype → validation → IP → production transfer |
| **C. Exploratory Research** (regulation, market, technology, sector feasibility — may be cross-sector) · *SOP: ABS-RND-SOP-016* | FRM-RND-16 → 17 | Brief (open) → findings report (close). **No** proven/not-proven verdict |

Streams A and B end in a **verdict** (proven / not proven). Stream C does **not** — it produces **options**, because exploratory research does not always resolve to a product decision.

## A. Product Validation stream

### FRM-RND-01 — Product Intake (Scouting)
- **Purpose:** Receive, register and pre-screen proposed external products before they enter validation.
- **Use when:** a new vendor/product is proposed and must be logged and screened.
- **Key sections:** Product Identity · Requirement · Initial Screening (disposition: proceed / park / reject).
- **Output → next:** if "proceed", go to FRM-RND-02. Recorded in FRM-RND-06.

### FRM-RND-02 — Vendor & Product Due Diligence Report
- **Purpose:** Examine credibility, legality and technical claims of a vendor/product before testing.
- **Use when:** a scouted product passed intake and needs verification.
- **Key sections:** Vendor · Compliance · Technical Claim Verification · Recommendation.
- **Output → next:** feeds FRM-RND-03 (test plan).

### FRM-RND-03 — Test Plan & Proven Criteria
- **Purpose:** Lock the test design and pass thresholds **before** testing so results stay objective.
- **Use when:** a product is cleared for testing; criteria must be fixed first.
- **Key sections:** Test Basis · Decision Rule (LOCKED) · Budget · Test Parameters & Pass Thresholds · Approval.
- **Note:** the decision rule is locked before testing — do not change thresholds after results are seen.

### FRM-RND-04 — Test Log
- **Purpose:** Record raw test data and evidence so results are repeatable and auditable.
- **Use when:** executing the tests defined in FRM-RND-03.
- **Key sections:** Test Configuration · Test Control (mandatory) · Measurement Results · Anomalies & Deviations from the Test Plan.

### FRM-RND-05 — Validation Report & Verdict
- **Purpose:** Convert test data into a formal decision (verdict) and a follow-up recommendation.
- **Use when:** testing is complete and a decision is required.
- **Key sections:** Summary · **Verdict (Proven / Proven with conditions / Not Proven)** · Recommendation · Claims vs Results · Approval.
- **Output → next:** result recorded in FRM-RND-06; recommendation may forward to Business Development.

### FRM-RND-06 — Product Validation Register
- **Purpose:** Master list of all validated products with their status and final verdict.
- **Use when:** maintaining the running record across all validations.
- **Key sections:** Administration · Validation List.

## B. In-House Development stream

### FRM-RND-10 — In-House Product Ideation & Requirements
- **Purpose:** Mature an internal product idea into validated requirements before development.
- **Key sections:** Idea · Initial Feasibility · Requirements & Decision · Approval.
- **Output → next:** if approved, go to FRM-RND-11.

### FRM-RND-11 — Development Plan & Gate Review
- **Purpose:** Control development through staged gates with a decision at each phase.
- **Key sections:** Plan · Design Control · Gate Decisions · Design Change Log.

### FRM-RND-12 — Prototype & Iteration Record
- **Purpose:** Record prototype builds and their refinement through iteration.
- **Key sections:** Prototype · Status · Iteration History.

### FRM-RND-13 — In-House Product Validation Report
- **Purpose:** Validate an internal product against requirements and standards before release.
- **Key sections:** Basis · Standards Compliance · **Verdict** · Test Results.

### FRM-RND-14 — Invention Disclosure & IP Register
- **Purpose:** Capture and protect intellectual property arising from product development.
- **Key sections:** Invention · IP Potential · Registration Status · Approval.

### FRM-RND-15 — Production Transfer & Commercialisation Package
- **Purpose:** Transfer a validated in-house product into production and commercial launch.
- **Key sections:** Prerequisites · Production & Pricing · Commercialisation & Review · Approval.

## C. Exploratory Research stream

### FRM-RND-16 — Exploratory Research Brief  *(opens the research)*
- **Purpose:** Set the direction, scope and key questions of an exploratory research activity before work begins. Covers research beyond product validation, including cross-sector topics (regulation, market, technology, sector feasibility).
- **Use when:** the group needs to research a question that is **not** about validating a specific vendor product — e.g. a new market, a regulation, a technology trend, or the feasibility of a new sector (such as an early mining/WAC feasibility study).
- **Key fields (mandatory *):** Background *, Research objective *, Decision to be supported *, Key questions 1–2 * (3–5 total), Included *, Excluded (boundary) *, Dimensions * (Regulatory / Market / Technical / Financial / Risk), Data sources *, **Research budget (if any) + Finance coordination**, Output form *, Audience *, Deadline *.
- **Budget & Finance:** if the research needs a budget, R&D records it on the form and places the details in **05-RND Shared**; Finance reads it there (2-way coordination, 1-way data flow, per ABS-GEN-SOP-004). R&D does not access the Finance folder.
- **Governing SOP:** ABS-RND-SOP-016.
- **Entity field** accepts: ABS / APW / BSN / WAC / **Cross-sector / Undetermined** — so it can hold research that does not (yet) belong to a single unit.
- **Link:** carries a **Reference No. `RSCH-YYYY-NNN`** that ties it to its findings report (FRM-RND-17).
- **Output → next:** when research completes, results go into FRM-RND-17 with the same Reference No.

### FRM-RND-17 — Research Findings Report  *(closes the research)*
- **Purpose:** Report findings, analysis, implications and options from the exploratory research. **Unlike FRM-RND-05, it does NOT issue a proven / not-proven verdict** — it presents options; the decision stays with Direksi.
- **Use when:** an FRM-RND-16 research activity is finished and results must be reported.
- **Key fields:** Executive Summary * · Findings (one row per key question from RND-16) · Analysis (interpretation *, opportunities, obstacles, remaining uncertainties) · Implications by Dimension (Regulatory / Market / Technical / Financial / Risk) · **Recommendations = options** (e.g. proceed to feasibility study / form a unit / postpone / stop) · Limits & Verification (high-risk legal/licensing items needing human expert confirmation) · Follow-up · Approval (human gate).
- **Link:** its **Reference No. must equal** the FRM-RND-16 that opened the research.
- **Governing SOP:** ABS-RND-SOP-016.

## Decision guide (intent → form)

- "A vendor is offering a product / we found a product" → **FRM-RND-01** (then 02 → 03 → 04 → 05, logged in 06).
- "Is this vendor/product credible and legal?" → **FRM-RND-02**.
- "Define how we will test it / set pass criteria" → **FRM-RND-03**.
- "Record the test results" → **FRM-RND-04**.
- "Give the final verdict on a tested product" → **FRM-RND-05**.
- "Show the list of all validated products" → **FRM-RND-06**.
- "We have our own product idea" → **FRM-RND-10** (then 11 → 12 → 13; 14 for IP; 15 for launch).
- "Research a market / regulation / technology / new-sector feasibility (not a specific product)" → **FRM-RND-16**, then report with **FRM-RND-17**.
- "Early feasibility study for the mining business (WAC) before it becomes a unit" → **FRM-RND-16** (Entity = WAC; dimensions = Regulatory + Market + Technical + Risk), reported in **FRM-RND-17**.

## Key rules for the agent

- Product validation (A) and in-house development (B) end in a **verdict**; exploratory research (C) ends in **options**, never a verdict.
- **RND-16 and RND-17 are always paired by `RSCH-YYYY-NNN`.** If asked about research findings, check that a matching RND-16 exists.
- A completed form is **not** an authorisation to act — approval and sign-off are the **human gate**; the decision remains with Direksi.
- Governing SOPs: **ABS-RND-SOP-001** (forms 01-06, 10-15) and **ABS-RND-SOP-016** (forms 16-17, Exploratory Research).
